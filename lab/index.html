All measures shown here are exploratory geometric diagnostics. They cannot establish that either face is readable, that an illustration is Whistler-quality, or that a proposed mutation improves an observer's interpretation.

Parity / negative space / local weight

Opposite-sector ink balance

Three-by-three mass fractions and four opposite-sector residuals. The centre sector maps to itself. Inspired by PRC R-SECT, without claiming weight optimization improves faces.
