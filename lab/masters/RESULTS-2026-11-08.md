# Whistler historical reference corpus — first reproducible geometric results

**Date:** 2026-11-08.
**GitHub Actions run:** https://github.com/ClaudioDrews/reversible-faces-benchmark/actions/runs/42450936991 (PASS).
**Artifact:** \`whistler-reference-corpus\` (ZIP, downloadable from Actions run; includes historical source bytes, SVGs, re-renders, controlled perturbations, 256 radial/quadrant measurements and an interactive annotation interface).

## Confirmed source and processing outcomes

The runner fetched four Commons works and recorded their exact SHA-256s and source metadata. Cropping produces ONE canonical 512×512 raster per historical drawing. The opposite reproduction panel was reserved for independent source correspondence checks; it was never separately traced or fused.

Each representation is generated by one executable code path, then *re-rendered* and compared to the canonical source. SSIM is structural image similarity. It cannot prove recognition, subtle artistic craftsmanship, perceived head pose or semantic role switching.

The **tonal SVG** is consistently more similar to the grayscale source than either binary or contour SVG for all four source reproductions. This is a descriptive finding on four examples; no causal or population-level conclusion follows. Detailed hatch shading and grayscale gradients do not transfer perfectly even in the tonal output. The original sources must remain the authority.

The paired-image rotation correlations are extremely high but **not independent human-reading observations**. They verify that the paired reproductions are very similar after geometric rotation and minor translation correction. Differences remain in crop, scan, signature and possibly image processing.

## Data generated

- Four exact downloaded JPGs, untouched.
- Four canonical A rasters and four opposite-panel reference rasters.
- Twelve SVGs: one binary, one multi-tone, one thin contour derived **from each A**.
- Twelve re-renders of those SVGs (for fidelity comparisons).
- Thirty-two controlled grayscale perturbations (blur, random small gaps, ink dilation and erosion, independently for page-centred core and page-centred outside region). Each records actual changed pixels and keeps original untouched.
- 256 physical regional rows: four works × four renderings × four radial rings × four page quadrants. CSV includes dark-ink occupancy, mean ink, edge fraction and high-frequency local contrast.
- Interactive HTML offline inspector that compares layers/variants, rotates exactly 0–180°, shows quadrants/radial guides, allows **manual** semantic role and face-centre annotation, and exports CSV with page- and viewer-relative coordinates.

## Initial falsification/control lessons

1. **Vector fidelity is not equivalent to successful dual interpretation.** The source vs derived quantitative differences are nonzero in all three tracing methods, but no blinded face read has been collected.
2. **A simple page-centred radial measure is confounded by whitespace and head position.** In the four rasters, the outermost ring is mostly white; it would be incorrect to infer that “the periphery is more vague” directly from total page darkness. The manual annotation protocol explicitly adds estimated face centre for each upright reading.
3. **High-frequency ink does not imply semantic specificity.** The physical high-pass and edge maps are measurable; “beard”, “eye”, “hat”, “nose”, and whether a mark simultaneously supports both readings are still human judgement labels.
4. **The original raster is not interchangeable with binary ink.** Contour and binary reductions alter the appearance of facial volumes and hatching. “Line incompleteness” and “lost tonal information” should be manipulated separately.
5. **No-op edits count.** Perturbations export changed-pixel counts and source hashes; a physical difference is still not an improved B-face.
6. **Pose diversity remains open.** These historical references are examples, not a representative distribution of frontal/profile/downward heads. Future pose-specific analysis should not treat the artists' originals as a proof that one pose type is generally optimal.

## Evidence status, preserved scope

- **EXACT:** C₂ transform and coordinate mapping; raster parity; registration computations under their defined algorithm; measured SHA-256s.
- **MEASURED ON THIS CORPUS:** sample-pair correlations, vector re-render fidelity, physical radial/quadrant profile, changed pixels from perturbations.
- **HYPOTHESIS ONLY:** eye/mouth/nose tolerance regions; radial “vagueness” by semantic role; multistability/incompletion; head yaw/pitch/roll switching; figure-ground ownership; dual-functional lines; relationship between ornament detail and human reading.
- **NOT YET RUN:** calibrated face feature detection, human Bubbles psychophysics, observer role/pose labels, statistical generalization, visual LLM generation/critique tuned from empirical tolerances, expert artistic quality ratings.

**All hypotheses remain eligible** in [H01–H48 matrix](HYPOTHESIS-MATRIX.md). Opposed explanations (outward-increasing, constant or inward-increasing spatial tolerance) are retained until a controlled study can compare them on held-out designs.

## Next experiment gates

1. Inspect crop quality on every source; independently annotate perceived A/B landmarks, face centre and jaw/head axes on both orientations, avoiding expected names.
2. Compare two or more observers' labels; preserve disagreements and confidence.
3. Match actual ink area/changed-pixel burden between central and external perturbations before comparing perceptual effects.
4. Run controlled path-level and multi-scale omission trials, not only seeded grayscale deletion.
5. Measure task-specific human recognition before fitting statistical tolerance surfaces.
6. Prompt multiple LLMs with fixed briefs/budgets to generate original drawings under differing hypothesis portfolios, with an open unconstrained condition.
7. Evaluate separately the **weaker-face** recognition, dual-use contour roles, narrative, and art quality; retain all failed and unchanged candidates.

The official RFB benchmark's original historical results and the live GitHub Pages site were not modified by this experiment.
