What is preserved, what is still a hypothesis

These images let us examine geometry and graphic information across all recorded alternatives: shared landmarks or profile-specific features, down/side face pose, selective incompleteness, spatial tolerance distributions, figure/ground, contrast and hatching, radial central/peripheral detail, C₂, harmonic fields, contour grouping, salience, semantic role reassignment, and a nonverbal story.
